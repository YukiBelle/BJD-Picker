About BJD Picker

Your guide to Chinese BJD collections

What is BJD Picker?

BJD Picker is a comprehensive database and discovery platform for Ball-Jointed Doll (BJD) boxed sets.

We help collectors and enthusiasts find possible placements in blind box sets.

Special thanks & Disclaimer

This list is thanks to the community that watch BJD live unboxings. We could not have it without people volunteering to try and figure out what goes where.

That said, you accept the risk of not getting the one you want. Between shops handling boxes, manufacturers, and other unforseen circumstances there is never a 100% guarantee.

Our goal is to inform others of possible patterns that have been observed in unboxings. We want to help everyone have the best shot of getting their intended target! We will do our best to keep this site updated.
