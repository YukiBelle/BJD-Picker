About BJD Picker

Your guide to Chinese BJD collections

What is BJD Picker?

BJD Picker is a comprehensive database and discovery platform for Chinese Ball-Jointed Doll (BJD) boxed sets. We help collectors and enthusiasts find the perfect sets for their collections.

Our Mission

To make it easy for BJD fans to explore, search, and discover boxed sets from Chinese manufacturers. We provide detailed information about characters, positions, and rarity to help you make informed choices.

Features

  • Search for sets by name or character
  • Browse newly added collections
  • Explore popular and trending sets
  • Learn character positions in each set
  • Detailed set information and descriptions
